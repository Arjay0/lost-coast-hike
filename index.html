Lost Coast Trail · King Range Wilderness

Mattole to Black Sands

Friday Sep 11 – Monday Sep 14, 2026 · southbound · ~24.6 miles · 3 nights on the beach · all times PDT.
Tides: NOAA Shelter Cove station 9418024, verified against NOAA 8/31. Sep 11 is a new moon — spring tides, so the water rises and falls fast.

The one rule

Two stretches of this trail flood at high tide. We enter a tide zone only when the tide is below 3 feet and falling. The gate times below are computed for our dates — if we're early to a gate, we wait. And never turn your back on the ocean: this coast is known for sneaker waves.

Plan B — Sunday dawn crossing (we decide Saturday night)

Instead of waiting for 4 pm Sunday: leave Big Flat at first light (6:15 am), enter zone B at Miller Flat 6:45 right at the low, and push all 4 miles to Gitchell by 9:15–9:30, when the window closes. Payoff: we either camp at Gitchell and Monday has no tide gates at all, or we walk out Sunday by ~12:30 — a day early. The catch: this crossing rides a rising tide, so it only runs if the surf is GREEN and the group held 1.5 mph on Saturday. Hard rule: not past Buck Creek by 8:45 am → we camp at Buck and use Monday's window as planned. Nobody races the ocean.

Surf: go / no-go

  • GREEN — surf ≤ 6 ft, period under 13 s: normal care.
  • CAUTION — 6–10 ft or swell period ≥ 13 s (long-period swell = sneaker waves even when it looks mellow): deepest hour of each window only, enter early.
  • NO-GO — over 10 ft or any NWS High Surf / Beach Hazards alert: we stay in camp and wait a cycle. Every camp has water; nobody gets hurt waiting.

Before the drive up, check the Cape Mendocino buoy (height + period) and weather.gov/eka for alerts. On the trail we judge by eye: swash reaching the base of the slope inside a window means it's a CAUTION day.

Water

  • Carry 1–1.5 L between creeks — they come every 1.5–3 miles.
  • Top off before each zone: Sea Lion Gulch (Fri), Big Flat (Sun).
  • Fill ~50 yd upstream of creek mouths (brackish at the mouth). Brown tannin tint is normal — filter and drink.

Good to know

  • Bear canister required (BLM) — rangers check at Mattole. All food, toiletries, and trash go in it at night.
  • Expect no campfires (September restrictions).
  • No cell signal from Mattole until Black Sands. Tell your people: check-in from Shelter Cove around noon Monday.
  • Marine layer soaks everything overnight — bring a pack towel, dry your fly at lunch.
  • Boots + gaiters beat trail runners here; camp shoes are worth their weight for the creek mouths.

Window edges are estimates (±20 min) — the falling-tide rule is the real margin. The spring window decays through the trip: Friday has the deepest lows, Monday the weakest, which is exactly why the plan front-loads the miles and crosses most of zone B on Sunday evening.
